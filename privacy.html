Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Cole's Auto Detailing provides car cleaning and residential window-cleaning services in the Alvin, Texas area. We respect the personal information customers share when requesting service.

Information submitted to us

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the name, telephone number, optional email address, preferred service date, requested service, and any details you choose to share. If you become a customer, we may keep records of your services, appointment details, amounts paid, and follow-up preferences.

How information is used

We use customer information to respond to inquiries, provide estimates, schedule and perform services, maintain business records, and contact customers about requested work. We do not ask visitors to enter payment-card numbers in the contact form.

Service providers

Our website and business records use Supabase for database and authentication services and GitHub Pages for website hosting. Where online payment links are offered, checkout is handled by Stripe, which processes payment data under its own privacy practices. Links and ratings from Google Maps may be shown on this website using Google Maps Platform services. These services may process technical information such as device and network information subject to their own policies. Please review Google's Privacy Policy, Stripe's Privacy Policy, and Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Information requests

For questions about information you have submitted, or to request a correction or deletion when appropriate, call (832) 877-5556. We may retain records when needed for payment reconciliation, business administration, or applicable legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as our website and services change. The update date above will reflect substantial changes.
